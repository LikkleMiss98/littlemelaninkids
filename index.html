Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

LittleMelaninKids ("we", "us") runs a small, private tool called Income Engine. Its only job is to publish videos we create to our own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, the basic profile information of our own TikTok account (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account.
  • YouTube and Instagram: permission to upload videos to our own channels, reply to comments on our own posts, and read view and like counts for our own posts.

How we use it

We use this access only to publish our own videos, read performance numbers for our own posts, and reply to comments on our own posts. We do not access other people's accounts, send messages, follow, like, or comment on other accounts automatically.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored on our own computer and are never shared. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. We delete stored tokens when we disconnect an account; you can also revoke access at any time from the platform's own app settings.

Children's privacy

Our videos are made for children, but we never collect personal information from children. We do not ask viewers for names, ages, photos, or locations, and our YouTube videos are marked "made for kids" as required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our TikTok and Instagram accounts are run by an adult and speak to parents.
